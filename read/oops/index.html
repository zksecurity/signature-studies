OOPS / AGM

With degree d fixed for the game, forgery probability is at most goodEpsilon + badEpsilon + 1 / |F|. The final target topic may have at most d distinct signed scalar messages.

OOPS.Statement.oops_lazy_agm_of_dlog_machine_hardness · revision b0b0230 · Lean 4.34.0. Original module · Compiler report.

The adversary queries topic/degree hash bases and scalar/topic signatures. Repeated messages and other topics still consume signing resources. Two DLog hardness assumptions use the derived good- and bad-branch machine budgets.

  2. Lines 32–33: adversary. Hash indices are topic/index pairs with d + 1 indices; signed messages are scalar/topic pairs.

  7. Lines 55–60: good hardness. Assume DLog hardness at the good solver budget, including degree-dependent weight costs.

  8. Lines 61–66: bad hardness. Apply a separate budget and allowance to the bad solver, charging its masked-base construction.

  9. Lines 67–71: conclusion. Bound valid fresh scalar/topic forgeries satisfying the distinct-message cap by both allowances and the residual field-size term.
